System Hardware Architecture

Indirect Mandagni Thermal Bath

A 500W element heats an outer water bath only. The inner SS-304 vessel containing the decoction stays capped at 85–90°C. No direct heating contact prevents thermal degradation and herb scorching.

Forced Circulation Loop

12V PWM-controlled food-grade silicone loop pushes water through a 710µm perforated stainless basket. Ensures complete extraction in ≤12 minutes without open boiling loss.

Dual Dosing Format (Pods & Sachets)

Supports both NTAG213 tagged single-use pods and reusable SS-304 fine mesh sachets holding 21g coarse Yavakuta powder.
